Pre-game picks

Analysis posts about 30 minutes before each team game and 90 minutes before the first fight on a card.

How picks are made and graded

Each game gets a facts packet (market line, rest, recent form, injuries when available). A small model nudges the no-vig market probability; the edge is the model’s win chance minus the market’s. A pick is a PLAY only when the edge, the data coverage and the price all clear the thresholds; otherwise it is a PASS and nothing is recorded.

Picks are locked when posted and never edited. A result is W, L or V (void: push, tie or draw on a moneyline, cancelled, or postponed more than 48 hours). Win % leaves voids out. Profit is in units at the price posted. Top picks are marked at posting time, up to five a day.

This is an experimental baseline that has not been backtested. Treat every pick as unproven until the record says otherwise.
